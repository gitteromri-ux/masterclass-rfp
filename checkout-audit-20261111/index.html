Checkout works end to end. It is slow, and Meta loses the buyer after the Enroll click.

Tested inside the Facebook app browser on three iPhones, from the homepage to a live Airwallex card form. Buyers wait about 10 seconds after Continue before they can type a card, and no Lead, AddToCart or InitiateCheckout event reaches Meta.

How it was tested

  • Cloud iPhone browser on the Safari engine, at iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 15 and iPhone 14 screen sizes.
  • Each phone carried the Facebook iOS app browser signature. Feed and Reels open the same in-app browser, so one run covers both.
  • Full flow on each phone: homepage, Enroll, your details, plan and date, payment page, card form loaded. No card entered, nothing charged.
  • Timings ran on a fast cloud connection. On 4G inside the Facebook app expect slower.
  • This is a simulation, not BrowserStack physical phones.

Meta tracking, Pixel and CAPI, step by step

Every Pixel and CAPI call was captured and stopped at the network edge, so no test event reached Meta. Click ID came in through a test fbclid.

Landing: PageView and ViewContent
Pixel and CAPI both fire with the same event ID, so they deduplicate. fbp and fbc present, fbclid kept in exact case. Match keys sent on CAPI.
Enroll click: Enroll_CTA_Click and Clicked_on_Enroll
Pixel and CAPI both fire, event IDs match.
Step 1 Continue, lead created: no Lead event
Only Meta's automatic SubscribedButtonClick. Nothing on CAPI.
Step 2 Continue, plan chosen: no AddToCart
Nothing on Pixel or CAPI.
Payment page and card form loaded: no InitiateCheckout
Nothing on Pixel or CAPI in the 15 seconds after the card form was ready. It may be wired to the Pay button. Only a real purchase can show that.
Purchase: not tested
Fires only after a real Airwallex or PayPal payment.

Meta optimises on the events it receives. With nothing between the Enroll click and Purchase, the ad sets cannot learn who starts checkout.

What to fix

  • Load the payment form during step 2, so the card fields are ready the moment the buyer lands. Today the price box reads Confirming tuition for about 5 seconds, then the card form stays blank for about 5 more.
  • Fire Lead on step 1, AddToCart on step 2 and InitiateCheckout when the payment page opens, on both Pixel and CAPI, with the same event ID and the CRM order ID.

What works

  • Price is right on every screen: $79 today, then 4 times $169, $755 total.
  • Promo code JULIE249 applies, and the Julie VIP masterclass shows as free.
  • One lead is sent per submit, with no duplicates.
  • Click ID and browser ID are captured correctly on landing.
